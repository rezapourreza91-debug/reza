## 2. Specimen geometry: two cases, both fixed now

Source of the FE geometry: `_SERVER_NEW/J_1104.inp` (frame and both filler parts) and the cell

file a closed manifold). At 1.04 g/cm3 their masses for P1-P4 equal the FE model masses in the

| | Case A: printed from `STL_FEgeom/` | Case B: printed from `STL/` |
|---|---|---|
| target mass, P2/P4 | 47.92 g | 48.97 g |
| target mass, P1/P3/P5/P6 | 47.83 g | 48.97 g |

Which case applies is recorded per specimen by the sha256 of the STL file sent to the printer

(section 6, S2 and S4) is reported with the 2.2-2.4 % solid-area difference stated next to it, and
no level agreement or disagreement is attributed to the material or the model alone. Rank criteria
are the same in both cases.

## 3. Before testing, for every specimen
1. Label with the specimen ID of section 4 and the STL sha256 (first 8 characters).
2. Weigh to 0.01 g. Measure height (loading direction), width and depth with a caliper, three
   readings each. Photograph both faces.
3. Mass rule: a specimen outside +/-3 % of its target mass (section 2) is still tested, flagged,
   and left out of the primary analysis; every result is also reported with it included.
4. Record printer, filament (brand, colour, lot), nozzle and bed temperature, layer height,
   extrusion width, perimeters, print orientation and print date. All lattice specimens and coupons
   come from one spool.

## 4. Test order (fixed now)
Generated with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261010).permutation` over the 21 IDs:

`P4U-3 P3-2 P5-3 P3-1 P4U-2 P2-1 P5-2 P4U-1 P2-2 P5-1 P6-1 P1-3 P4I-3 P4I-2 P6-2 P1-1 P2-3 P1-2 P4I-1 P3-3 P6-3`

A specimen that is not available is skipped; the order of the rest is kept.

## 5. Test conduct
Quasi-static compression between flat, unlubricated steel platens, no adhesive (the FE platens are
tied to the frame; this difference is reported). Crosshead speed 5 mm/min, stroke 20 mm or 90 % of
the load-cell capacity, whichever comes first. Load cell >= 50 kN. Force and crosshead
displacement at >= 10 Hz. Video of the lattice face for the whole test. No specimen is retested.
The machine-compliance curve (platens in contact, same speed, to 30 kN) is recorded once.

## 6. Data processing and analyses (fixed now)
Processing: force-displacement from the crosshead, displacement zeroed where the force first
reaches 5 N; no compliance correction in
the primary analysis (with the measured compliance curve as a sensitivity check). Metrics, with the
definitions used for the FE data:
- EA_dd (energy from 0 to the densification displacement d_dens, the v4 definition in the GP01/GP02
  registrations), F_plat, ULC and IPCF: `extract_outputs_v4.py --height 49.13`. IPCF also under the
  other two definitions of `_handoff/reports/H1_report.md` (knee_all and pre-0.05H). Any change to the script for experimental curves is reported.
- EA16: energy from 0 to 16 mm (fixed stroke, as in `PLAN_experimental_validation_v1.md`), by
  trapezoidal integration of the same processed curve; SEA16 = EA16 / measured mass.

Registered analyses:
- **P (primary, unchanged).** The GP01 and GP02 rank and level tests exactly as written in their
  registrations, on mean EA_dd.
- **S1 (rank at fixed stroke).** Spearman rho between mean experimental EA16 and FE EA16 over the
  tested FE designs (P1, P2, P3, P4U, and P4I if tested); and Spearman rho over all tested designs
  using FE EA_dd for P1-P4 and the registered GP prediction for P5/P6. Reported with n and the
  exact chance probability of the observed rho.
- **S2 (level).** k_i = mean EA_exp / EA_FE per FE design, their mean and range, for EA_dd and EA16.
  Descriptive; no pass bar.
- **S3 (upright vs inverted).** Mean EA_dd and EA16 of P4I minus P4U with a 95 % t-interval,
  compared with the FE difference (1094 vs 1093: 77.9 J vs 62.4 J, +25 %).
- **S4 (curves).** NRMSE of the mean experimental force-displacement curve against the FE curve,
  0-16 mm, per FE design. Descriptive.
- **S5 (deformation).** Video frames at 2, 4, 8, 12 and 16 mm next to FE frames. Descriptive.
- **S6 (scatter).** Coefficient of variation of EA16 within each design.

## 7. Expected pass probability of the primary rank tests (computed now)
Monte Carlo, 200,000 draws (`power_mc.py`, seed 20261010). True FE-equivalent EA: P1-P4 at their FE
values; GP01 ~ N(190.1, 12.5) and GP02 ~ N(136.3, 11.1) J, the registered predictions. A common
level factor cancels. Mean of 3 specimens per design, specimen scatter with coefficient of
variation cv.

| cv | GP01 between P2 and P1 | GP02 between P3 and P2 | rho = 1, five designs | rho = 1, six designs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 % | 0.93 | 0.93 | 0.93 | 0.87 |
| 10 % | 0.83 | 0.85 | 0.83 | 0.70 |
| 15 % | 0.70 | 0.74 | 0.70 | 0.51 |

- With the registered known bias of the GP model (EA > 165 J under-predicted by 9.3 J on average)
  and cv 10 %, GP01 passes with probability 0.75.
- With the GP prediction taken as exact (specimen scatter alone), cv 10 %: GP01 0.91, GP02 0.96.
- Two designs whose true EA differs by 10 % (5 %) are ordered correctly by the means of 3 specimens
  with probability 0.88 (0.73) at cv 10 %.
- The "partial pass" rho >= 0.9 of the GP01 registration is reached in >= 0.99 of draws at every cv
  above, because one swap of neighbours gives rho = 0.9 with five designs. It is reported, but it
  carries almost no evidence and is not called a pass in the paper.
So a failed primary test is a likely outcome even if the model is right, and it is reported as such.

## 8. Material coupons
Five tensile (ISO 527-2 type 1BA) and five compression (ASTM D695 prism) coupons from the same
spool, tested with the lattices, at 1 mm/min with an extensometer for the tensile modulus. They
calibrate a material card for a later FE run of the printed geometry. They do not enter P or S1-S6.

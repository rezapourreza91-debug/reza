## Why a second blind design
GP01 is new but sits 6 cells from the mirror images of runs 1113 / 1121 / 1122.
GP02 is the "completely new" check requested on 2026-10-06: it is at least 8 cells
(Hamming) from every grid33 design simulated or proposed in H:\claude, from each one's
left-right mirror, up-down flip and 180-degree rotation, and from GP01 (10 cells).
Its descriptors stay inside the training range on purpose: the paper claims rank transfer
inside the design space, not extrapolation beyond it.

## Model
Same as GP01: `inverse_design_v4.Surrogate`, unchanged, trained on UNIFIED_DATASET_v4.xlsx
(201 rows, SW_33_SIN quarantined), seed 0. Family-A 5-fold CV: EA_J R2 0.79, MAE 9.6 J.
Selection: 200,000 random 10-of-33 patterns, novelty filter (>= 8 from all 893 vectors),
then the candidate whose prediction sits furthest, in sd units, from both neighbouring
ladder designs P3 (1016, 114.3 J) and P2 (1104, 159.6 J).

   P4 (1093) < P3 (1016) < **P6 (GP02)** < P2 (1104) < P5 (GP01) < P1 (1097).
   Pass for GP02: it lies strictly between 1016 and 1104. Spearman over the six designs = 1.0

2. **Level after one-shot calibration.** k = mean(EA_exp / EA_FE) over P1-P4.
   Pass: |EA_exp(GP02) - k * 136.3| <= 2 * k * 11.1 J.
3. **Raw level** is reported, not claimed (FE over-predicted force ~15 % on the 5x5 specimen).

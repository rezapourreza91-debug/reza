That row becomes a solid band, so the specimen behaves as a four-row lattice instead of a
five-row one, unlike every other design in the set. Raised by Ali Yadegari on 2026-10-10.
Its upside-down twin 1094 (whole top row) and runs 1095 and 1103 share the defect.
